# Changelog

Based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-23

### Added
- Material 3 HCT color token engine (`materialyoucolor`) with Dark, Light, AMOLED modes, 9 scheme variants, contrast levels, and per-role overrides
- 19 curated presets across 10 color families: Brown, Red, Blue, Yellow, Green, Pink, Purple, Orange, Black, and White
- OS Dynamic Color sync (`m3vlc sync` / `--watch`) from Windows and Linux wallpapers or accent colors
- Local Theme Studio (`m3vlc studio`) with per-role color pickers, live preview, and one-click VLC restart
- HiDPI vector builds (`1x`, `1.25x`, `1.5x`, `2x`) with surface-matched opaque sprites
- Full VLC Skins2 suite: Main player, docked playlist side sheet, compact mini-player, 10-band equalizer, control center, fullscreen HUD, about dialog, and contextual audio/video menus
- VLC `skin.dtd` validator, reproducible `.vlt` packager, preview compositor, and cross-platform installer
